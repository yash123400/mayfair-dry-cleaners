Our Story

About Mayfair Dry Cleaners

Located in Mayfair at the heart of Westminster, we have been providing excellent service on the premises since 1967. Our staff are SATRA trained and qualified to ensure you're receiving the very best in laundry and tailoring services.

Every item that passes through our doors is inspected, treated for stains, cleaned, hand finished and, if necessary, repaired or altered. We always like to go that extra mile for our customers.

Customers consistently commend our quick turnaround, friendly staff, and ability to restore garments to like-new condition. Our professional team provides efficient alterations and meticulous care, making us a popular choice in Mayfair.
